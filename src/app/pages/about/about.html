About

Hi, I'm Kenza. Nice to meet you.

I build web applications, APIs and data tools that people actually use. For more than seven years, I have worked with research labs and tech teams, turning complex needs into reliable software. Today, I bring that same rigor to companies, associations and teams of every size.

Kenza

My story

Science taught me rigor. Code lets me share it.

I started in biology, with a bachelor's degree between Casablanca and Bordeaux, then a master's in bioinformatics in Paris. Very quickly, what I enjoyed most was the code: automating analyses, organizing data, and building tools other people could rely on.

For seven years, I have worked with research labs and tech teams on pipelines, databases and web platforms. In 2023, I formalized my full-stack skills with a one-year Django certification, then applied them in production with Angular, Django REST and cloud deployment.

Science is part of my story, not a limit. I work for clients from every field, and what I bring from the lab is a method: understand first, test everything, document for the next person.
